01 the client

Krombacher Spezi is a premium cola-orange fusion imported to Georgia, a lifestyle drink known for its quality and unique taste. Its Georgian marketing framework worked so well that Krombacher officially added it to its global guidelines, making the Georgian approach an international benchmark.

02 my role

I run Spezi Georgia's TikTok end to end: developing the content strategy, filming and editing the videos every month, and managing the community around the account.

03 how it went

I launched the account from zero in June 2025 and grew it to 1,178 followers by October. The top video reached 403K views, 5,830 shares and 2,058 hours of total play time. Every one of the four videos above passed 120K views, adding up to 889K views and 12.5K likes.
