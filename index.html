About Me

Creator by passion,
producer by craft.

For more than three years I've been living and breathing short-form video. What started as experimenting with a phone and a ring light turned into a full-time craft — writing hooks, directing shoots, and editing content that connects with audiences in the first second.

I handle the entire production pipeline: concept, scripting, filming, editing, sound design and trend strategy. I've partnered with brands and creators to build campaigns that feel native to TikTok — authentic, fast-paced, and built to perform.

What drives me is simple: the challenge of telling a meaningful story in under 60 seconds, and the thrill of watching it reach millions.
