01 the client

Established in 2017, Citycom is Georgia's first fintech platform for residential communities. It turns traditional elevator systems into smart digital networks, replacing coin boxes with card payments and e-wallets, and gives residents' associations a transparent web portal to manage their own funds and tariffs.

02 my role

I run Citycom's TikTok end to end: developing the content strategy, filming and editing the videos every month, and managing the community around the account.

03 how it went

The account grew from 500 to 1,203 followers while I ran it. The top video reached 163.7K views, 4,784 shares and 604 hours of total play time. The second passed 73.5K views and was saved 934 times. Together these four videos brought in over 279K views and 7.2K likes.
