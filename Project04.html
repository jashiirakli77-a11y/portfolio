01 the client

Founded in 1938, Dinamo Tbilisi is the most decorated and historic water polo club in Georgia. After years of dominance in the Soviet era, the club was re-established in 2011 and has since become a key representative of Georgian water polo on the European stage.

02 my role

I run the club's TikTok end to end: developing the content strategy, filming and editing the videos every month, and managing the community around the account.

03 how it went

I made 40 videos for the club between September 2024 and July 2025. The top one reached 181K views, 7,430 likes and 3,319 shares, and all four videos above passed 100K views, adding up to 530K views.
