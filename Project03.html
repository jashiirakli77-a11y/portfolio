01 the client

The Dinamo Tbilisi Water Polo Academy was founded in 2011 by active Georgian national team players and is one of the leading places to grow water polo talent in Georgia. It takes children from the age of 3 and runs structured programs aligned with European standards, covering both swimming and water polo skills.

02 my role

I run the academy's TikTok end to end: developing the content strategy, filming and editing the videos every month, and managing the community around the account.

03 how it went

I made 48 videos for the academy between June 2024 and September 2025. The top one reached 145K views and 697 hours of total play time, and the second passed 113K views with 452 shares. Together the four videos above brought in 332K views and 7.1K likes.
