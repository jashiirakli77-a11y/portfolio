Making heating and cooling worth watching.

Celsiusi.ge is a heating and cooling store with a huge selection. I ran its TikTok from September 2025 to July 2026: developing the strategy, filming and editing the videos every month, and managing the community. The account grew from 0 to 2,733 followers, and the top video alone reached 203.6K views and brought in 804 new followers.
