the project ↓

One mic, one question, real people.

RC Cola (Royal Crown Cola) is a Southern cola with more than a century of history, still sweetened only with cane sugar, just like in 1905. Together with my co-worker I ran RC Cola Georgia's TikTok: developing the strategy, filming and editing the videos every month, and managing the community. We made 111 videos between May and November 2025 and grew the account from 0 to 12.7K followers. The street interviews above were the biggest hits, each passing 430K views.
